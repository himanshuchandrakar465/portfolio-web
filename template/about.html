About me

Building AI by understanding how it works.

I’m Himanshu Chandrakar, an aspiring AI/ML engineer interested in machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI. I enjoy turning what I learn into practical projects and applications.

A practical learner

From learning to building.

I focus on understanding the fundamentals behind AI and applying them through hands-on projects. My interests include deep learning, computer vision, LLM applications, RAG, and AI agents. I enjoy working through the complete process — from understanding the data and training models to building something people can actually use.

What keeps me engaged is the full path: understanding the data, selecting an approach, evaluating it carefully, and making the result easy to use. I’m particularly interested in scalable AI systems that are grounded in a clear user need.

I learn best by building, experimenting, and improving with every project.
